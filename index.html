Mercury gets its first visitor in years as a pair of spacecraft loop into orbit

Phys.org

After an eight-year journey through the inner solar system, two spacecraft travelling together have slipped into orbit around Mercury this weekend. Getting there is harder than it sounds: falling toward the Sun means constantly braking, which took a long series of planetary flybys. Now the pair will start working through the many open questions about the smallest, most battered planet.

Por que é legal: é o primeiro "visitante" de Mercúrio desde que a sonda Messenger encerrou sua missão em 2015.

Einstein's relativity just upended a fundamental rule of chemistry

ScienceDaily

Researchers at Brown University found direct evidence that relativistic effects — electrons in heavy atoms moving at a sizable fraction of light speed — change how bismuth forms triple bonds, blending bond types that textbooks keep separate. It confirms an old prediction that the usual bonding rules break down at the bottom of the periodic table.

Por que é legal: a relatividade não é só coisa de buraco negro — ela mexe até na química de um elemento que cabe na mão.

Wolves help wild blueberries grow, US study finds

Mongabay

In northern Minnesota, gray wolves eat a surprising amount of fruit — at peak season, berries can make up most of an adult's weekly diet. Researchers found that blueberry seeds that had passed through a wolf sprouted faster and more often than seeds taken straight from the bush. Since wolves roam far, they may be quietly planting blueberries across the forest.

Mongabay

Years of cleanup have moved the river from "severely polluted" to "moderately polluted", and the otters are a living sign that it is working.

Reasons to be Cheerful

Back in 1999, the Galician city of Pontevedra started handing its center back to pedestrians: fewer cars, lower speed limits, more space for people. Car trips in the center have since dropped by about 97%, and the city hasn't recorded a traffic death since 2011.

Superinteressante

Com a bateria quase cheia, os íons de lítio não conseguem se encaixar no grafite rápido o suficiente, e forçar a barra faz lítio metálico se depositar na superfície — o que gasta a bateria e, no limite, pode causar curto-circuito.

Por que é legal: aquela lentidão irritante é o celular cuidando de si mesmo.

23 de novembro de 1900 — nasce Mestre Bimba

Wikipédia

Manuel dos Reis Machado, o Mestre Bimba, nasceu em Salvador e criou a Capoeira Regional, organizando golpes, sequências de ensino e um método próprio. Em 1932 abriu a primeira academia de capoeira do país, ajudando a tirar a arte da marginalidade e levá-la ao mundo — hoje a roda de capoeira é Patrimônio Cultural Imaterial da Humanidade.
