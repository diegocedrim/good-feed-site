Ancient rocks from the Moon's far side rewrite the Solar System's violent past

ScienceDaily

Samples brought back from the far side of the Moon suggest that asteroid impacts in the early Solar System didn't come as one catastrophic "late heavy bombardment," but tapered off gradually over billions of years. Since the Moon is a near-perfect record of what was hitting Earth too, the finding changes the picture of the environment in which life first appeared here.
